TripKap Support

TripKap splits trip costs between friends: offline, any currency, and a clear "who pays whom" at the end. For help, write to support@spotterhk.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Common questions

Can friends use the app together on their own phones? Not in this version: one person keeps the books on their iPhone. Add everyone's name to the trip, record each expense (who paid, who shares), and when you settle up send the group the summary (picture or text) or the full statement (CSV or PDF) from ⋯ → Share & Export.

Can I check every record? Yes. The statement lists every expense with who paid and who shares, every repayment, and each person's account. Deleted records are kept under ⋯ → Deleted Records, where you can restore them.

Does TripKap move money? No. It only works out who owes whom and builds payment links or QR codes that open your payment app. It never handles card or bank details.

Can I settle in another currency, or in several? Yes. On the Settle Up screen choose Show in to see the amounts in another currency, and record a repayment in one or more currencies; each part keeps its own rate.

How do I delete my data? Everything is stored on your phone only: delete the app to remove it.

常見問題

朋友可以用各自部手機一齊記帳嗎？ 呢個版本唔得：由一個人喺自己部 iPhone 記晒帳。先喺旅程加入所有人嘅名字，逐筆記低開支（誰付款、誰分擔）， 結算時由「⋯ → 分享與匯出」將摘要（圖片或文字）或完整對帳單（CSV 或 PDF）傳俾大家。

點樣核對每一筆記錄？ 對帳單會列出每筆開支（誰付款、誰分擔）、所有還款同各人帳目；刪除咗嘅記錄會保留喺「⋯ → 已刪除記錄」，可以還原。

TripKap 會經手金錢嗎？ 唔會。佢只計算誰欠誰，並生成會打開你收款／轉帳 App 嘅連結或 QR，完全唔處理銀行卡或戶口資料。

可以用其他貨幣結算，或者混合幾種貨幣嗎？ 可以。喺「結算」畫面揀「以…顯示」睇其他貨幣嘅金額；記錄還款時可以加多個貨幣部分，每部分有自己嘅匯率。

點樣刪除我嘅資料？ 所有資料只存放喺你部手機：刪除 App 就會全部移除。
