TripKap Support

TripKap splits trip costs between friends: offline, any currency, and a clear "who pays whom" at the end. For help, write to support@spotterhk.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Common questions

Can friends use the app together on their own phones? Not in this version: one person keeps the books on their iPhone. Add everyone's name to the trip, record each expense (who paid, who shares), and when you settle up send the group the summary (picture or text) or the full statement (CSV or PDF) from ⋯ → Share & Export.

Can I check every record? Yes. The statement lists every expense with who paid and who shares, every repayment, and each person's account. Deleted records are kept under ⋯ → Deleted Records, where you can restore them. The statement also carries a Books Check: whether what was paid, what was shared, the repayments and the balances add up.

What if I change or lose my phone? Tap Back Up This Trip in ⋯ → Share & Export, or Back Up All Trips in Settings, and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. Open it with TripKap on the new phone to restore everything (deleted records and receipt photos included). Restoring never replaces what is already on the phone: a trip that is already there is added as a copy.

Does TripKap move money? No. It only works out who owes whom and builds payment links or QR codes that open your payment app. It never handles card or bank details.

Can I settle in another currency, or in several? Yes. On the Settle Up screen choose Show in to see the amounts in another currency, and record a repayment in one or more currencies; each part keeps its own rate.

How do I delete my data? Everything is stored on your phone only: delete the app to remove it.

TripKap 支援

TripKap 幫朋友分旅行開支：離線可用、任何貨幣，並在最後清楚列出誰轉給誰。 如需協助，請電郵至 support@spotterhk.com，並註明 iPhone 型號及 iOS 版本。

常見問題

朋友可以用各自的手機一起記帳嗎？ 這個版本不可以：由一個人在自己的 iPhone 上記錄所有帳目。請先在旅程中加入所有人的名字，逐筆記下開支（誰付款、誰分擔），結算時由「⋯ → 分享與匯出」把摘要（圖片或文字）或完整對帳單（CSV 或 PDF）傳給大家。

如何核對每一筆記錄？ 對帳單會列出每筆開支（誰付款、誰分擔）、所有還款及各人的帳目，並附上「帳目核對」：已付款項、分擔款項、還款及結餘的合計是否吻合。刪除了的記錄會保留在「⋯ → 已刪除記錄」，可以還原。

換手機或遺失手機，資料怎麼辦？ 請在「⋯ → 分享與匯出」按「備份此旅程」，或在「設定」按「備份所有旅程」，把備份檔存到「檔案」或 iCloud Drive。在新手機用 TripKap 打開該檔案，即可還原（包括已刪除記錄和收據相片）。還原不會取代手機上已有的資料；如旅程已經存在，會以副本加入。

TripKap 會經手金錢嗎？ 不會。它只計算誰欠誰，並產生可打開你收款或轉帳 App 的連結或 QR 碼，完全不處理銀行卡或戶口資料。

可以用其他貨幣結算，或混合幾種貨幣嗎？ 可以。在「結算」畫面選擇「以…顯示」，即可查看其他貨幣的金額；記錄還款時可以加入多個貨幣部分，每部分有自己的匯率。

如何刪除我的資料？ 所有資料只存放在你的手機上：刪除 App 即會全部移除。
