TripKap Support

TripKap splits trip costs between friends: offline, any currency, and a clear "who pays whom" at the end. For help, write to support@spotterhk.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Common questions

How do I invite friends? Add everyone's name to the trip first. Then open the trip, tap the ⋯ menu and choose Invite Friends. Send the link (or the code, or show the QR code). Your friend opens it in TripKap and picks their name. A trip holds up to 8 people.

A friend's link does nothing. Open TripKap, tap Join a Trip on the trip list and paste the link or type the code.

Does TripKap move money? No. It only works out who owes whom and builds payment links or QR codes that open your payment app. It never handles card or bank details.

Can I settle in another currency, or in several? Yes. On the Settle Up screen choose Show in to see the amounts in another currency, and record a repayment in one or more currencies; each part keeps its own rate.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app to remove everything on your phone. To erase what you put in the cloud (shared trips), open Settings → Cloud → Delete My Cloud Data.

TripKap 支援

TripKap 幫朋友分旅行開支：離線可用、任何貨幣、最後清楚列出誰轉給誰。 需要協助請電郵 support@spotterhk.com，並請註明 iPhone 型號同 iOS 版本。

常見問題

點樣邀請朋友？ 先喺旅程入面加入所有人嘅名字，再開旅程，撳右上角「⋯」揀「邀請朋友」，把連結（或邀請碼、QR）傳俾朋友， 朋友喺 TripKap 打開後揀返自己嘅名字就得。一個旅程最多 8 人。

朋友撳連結冇反應？ 打開 TripKap，喺旅程列表撳「加入旅程」，貼上連結或輸入邀請碼。

TripKap 會經手金錢嗎？ 唔會。佢只計算誰欠誰，並生成會打開你收款／轉帳 App 嘅連結或 QR，完全唔處理銀行卡或戶口資料。

可以用其他貨幣結算，或者混合幾種貨幣嗎？ 可以。喺「結算」畫面揀「以…顯示」睇其他貨幣嘅金額；記錄還款時可以加多個貨幣部分，每部分有自己嘅匯率。

點樣刪除我嘅資料？ 刪除 App 會移除手機上所有資料；想清走上傳咗去雲端嘅共享旅程資料，去「設定 → 雲端 → 刪除我的雲端資料」。
