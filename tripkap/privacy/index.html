TripKap Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

TripKap is a travel expense-splitting app. It is built so that your trips stay on your phone, and leave it only when you choose to share a trip with friends.

What TripKap stores, and where

Everything you enter — trips, members' names, expenses, receipt photos, payment details of your friends (such as a PayPal.me name or FPS number) and settlement records — is stored on your device. TripKap needs no sign-up and no password. A trip stays only on your phone until you tap Share; trips you never share never reach any server.

Receipt photos you attach are copied into the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded, even in a shared trip. TripKap asks for camera access only to take those photos.

If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS. TripKap never receives your face data or passcode; it is only told whether the check succeeded.

Shared trips (optional)

When you share a trip, or join a friend's trip with an invite link or code, TripKap creates an anonymous account for your phone (a random identifier; no name, e-mail or phone number) and keeps a copy of the trip in a cloud database so that everybody in the trip sees the same records. The database is operated for us by Supabase and is hosted in Singapore.

What is stored in the cloud for a shared trip:

  • the trip's name, emoji, dates and currency;
  • the members' names and emojis, and the payment details entered for them (such as a PayPal.me name or FPS number);
  • every expense (title, amount, currency, exchange rate, category, date, note, who paid, who shares) and every repayment, exchange and kitty entry;
  • the trip's activity history: who added, changed or deleted what and when, with the values before and after;
  • the invite code and which anonymous accounts take part in the trip, and which member each one is.

Who can see it: only people who joined the trip with its invite. Everyone in a trip sees everything in it, including the members' payment details. The owner of the trip (the person who shared it) can remove people, replace the invite link and delete the trip. Anyone who knows an invite code can join until the owner replaces it, so share it only with the people you mean.

What is not stored in the cloud: receipt photos, your contacts, your location, advertising or device identifiers. Like any internet service, the hosting provider sees the IP address a request comes from.

How long it is kept:

  • a trip stays in the cloud until its owner deletes it; a deleted trip is erased for good after 30 days;
  • the activity history is erased 30 days after the trip's end date, unless the owner has TripKap Pro, in which case it is kept;
  • you can erase everything you put in the cloud yourself at any time (see "Your choices").

When you leave a shared trip or are removed from it, the copy on your phone stays, but it stops updating. Your name and the expenses you entered stay in the trip for the other people, because their balances depend on them.

What leaves your device

  • Exchange rates. About once a day TripKap downloads a table of market exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains no information about you or your trips; like any internet request, the provider can see your IP address.
  • Purchases. If you buy TripKap Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. TripKap learns only whether you have Pro; we receive no payment details or personal information.
  • Shared trips. Only if you share or join a trip: the data described above goes to the cloud database.
  • What you choose to share. When you share a summary image, text, PDF or CSV, or open a payment link, the content goes to the app you choose (Messages, a bank or payment app, and so on). TripKap only builds the link or code; it never moves money and never stores or transmits card or bank account numbers.

What TripKap does not do

No advertising. No tracking across other companies' apps and websites. No selling of personal data. Data in shared trips is used only to show it to the people in the trip; it is not used for advertising or profiling.

Children

TripKap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. People of any age can be members of a trip that an adult shares.

Your choices

Delete the app to delete everything stored on your phone. Settings → Cloud → Delete My Cloud Data erases everything you put in the cloud: the trips you own are deleted for everybody in them, you are removed from the trips you only joined (your name and what you entered stay for the others, no longer linked to you), and your anonymous account is deleted. Copies on your phone are kept. Revoke camera access in iOS Settings. For any other request about your data, write to the address below.
