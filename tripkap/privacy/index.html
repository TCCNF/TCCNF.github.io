TripKap Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

TripKap is a travel expense-splitting app. It is built so that your data stays on your phone.

What TripKap stores, and where

Everything you enter — trips, members' names, expenses, receipt photos, payment details of your friends (such as a PayPal.me name or FPS number), repayments and notes — is stored on your device. TripKap needs no sign-up, no account and no password, and it has no server of its own: nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

Receipt photos you attach are copied into the app's private storage on your device. TripKap asks for camera access only to take those photos.

If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS. TripKap never receives your face data or passcode; it is only told whether the check succeeded.

Like the data of other apps, TripKap's data can be part of your iPhone backup if you use one. That backup is handled by Apple, not by TripKap.

Backup files

TripKap can write your trips (with deleted records and receipt photos) into a backup file (.tripkap). The file is not encrypted. It goes only where you choose to save or send it, such as Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, Messages or Mail. Opening a backup file in TripKap reads it on your phone; nothing is uploaded. TripKap cannot delete backup files you have saved elsewhere: manage them yourself.

What leaves your device

  • Exchange rates. About once a day TripKap downloads a table of market exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains no information about you or your trips; like any internet request, the provider can see your IP address.
  • What you choose to share. When you share a summary image, text, PDF, CSV or backup file, or open a payment link, the content goes to the app you choose (Messages, a bank or payment app, and so on). TripKap only builds the link or code; it never moves money and never stores or transmits card or bank account numbers.

What TripKap does not do

No advertising. No tracking. No analytics. No accounts. No selling of personal data. No purchases.

Children

TripKap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Delete the app to delete everything stored on your phone (backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them). Revoke camera access in iOS Settings. For any other request about your data, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

TripKap 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

TripKap 是一款旅行分帳 App，設計原則是讓你的資料留在你自己的手機上。

TripKap 儲存什麼資料，儲存在哪裡

你輸入的所有內容——旅程、成員姓名、開支、收據相片、朋友的收款資料（例如 PayPal.me 名稱或 FPS 號碼）、還款及備註——都儲存在你的裝置上。TripKap 毋須註冊、毋須帳戶、毋須密碼，也沒有自己的伺服器：你輸入的內容不會傳送給我們，也不會傳送給任何其他人。

你附加的收據相片會複製到 App 在你裝置上的私有儲存空間。TripKap 只會為了拍攝這些相片而要求使用相機。

如你開啟 App 鎖，Face ID 或密碼由 iOS 驗證。TripKap 不會取得你的面容資料或密碼，只會得知驗證是否成功。

與其他 App 的資料一樣，如你有使用 iPhone 備份，TripKap 的資料可能包含在該備份之內。該備份由 Apple 處理，而非 TripKap。

備份檔案

TripKap 可以把你的旅程（連同已刪除的記錄和收據相片）寫成備份檔（.tripkap）。備份檔不經加密，只會儲存或傳送到你自己選擇的地方，例如「檔案」、iCloud Drive、AirDrop、訊息或電郵。在 TripKap 打開備份檔時，檔案只會在你的手機上讀取，不會上傳。TripKap 無法刪除你存放在其他地方的備份檔，請自行管理。

哪些資料會離開你的裝置

  • 匯率。 TripKap 約每日一次從 ExchangeRate-API（open.er-api.com）下載市場匯率表。請求不包含任何關於你或你的旅程的資料；與任何網絡請求一樣，服務供應商可以看到你的 IP 位址。
  • 你選擇分享的內容。 當你分享摘要圖片、文字、PDF、CSV 或備份檔，或打開收款連結時，內容會交到你所選擇的 App（例如訊息、銀行或付款 App）。TripKap 只負責產生連結或代碼；它不會轉移資金，也不會儲存或傳送銀行卡或銀行帳戶號碼。

TripKap 不會做的事

沒有廣告。沒有追蹤。沒有數據分析。沒有帳戶。不出售個人資料。沒有內購項目。

兒童

TripKap 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

你的選擇

刪除 App 即可刪除儲存在手機上的所有資料（你存放在其他地方的備份檔會留在原處）。你可以在 iOS 設定中撤銷相機權限。如對你的資料有任何其他要求，請寫信到下方地址。

修訂

如本政策有所更改，新版本會連同新的生效日期發佈於此網址。
