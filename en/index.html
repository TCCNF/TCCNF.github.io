Features
Everything for food and training in one app

From meal photos to workout logs and AI form checks, Spotter keeps it all in one place.

🍱

Meal logging with AI

Take a photo and get calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre. Or type what you ate, search a database of over 1,600 foods, or add your own. Track daily nutrition goals.

📸

AI form coach

Record or upload a clip of your set and the AI watches it and gives feedback on your form, for common lifts such as squats, push-ups and deadlifts.

📋

Training plans and logs

Over 230 exercises, filtered by body part, equipment and training type. Build your own plans, log weight and reps for every set, and mark supersets and drop sets.

💧

Water tracking

Log what you drink against a daily goal, with reminders at the times and intervals you choose.

💬

Ask the AI coach

Ask about training, nutrition and recovery at any time, in a full conversation. Save the answers that matter.

📊

Progress tracking

Charts for body weight, body fat and muscle mass. Best weight, reps and volume for every exercise, with personal records marked.

🏃

Cardio logging

Over 20 cardio types, including running, cycling, swimming, HIIT and hiking. Outdoor sessions record a GPS route map, and you can add sessions by hand.

❤️

Apple Health

Connect Apple Health to see today's steps and your latest heart rate on the home screen.

📲

InBody scan

Take a photo of your InBody report and the AI reads your weight, body fat and muscle mass and saves them to your profile.

🏠

Smart home screen

A personal dashboard with your weekly training ring, muscle recovery, a training calendar, daily energy check-in, Apple Health data and quick tools.

☁️

Cloud backup

Sign in and everything backs up to the cloud, so a new phone loses nothing. Works offline and syncs when you are back online.

🧘‍♂️

Warm-up and stretching

Step-by-step warm-ups before training and stretches after, to lower the risk of injury and help you recover.

🔗

Share and invite

Finish a workout and get a share card for your Instagram Story. Invite a friend and you both get extra AI uses.
