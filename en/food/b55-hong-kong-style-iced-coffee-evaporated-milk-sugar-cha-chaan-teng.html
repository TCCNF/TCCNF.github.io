Hong Kong-style iced coffee calories

Hong Kong-style iced coffee (1 cup) has about 130 kcal. Nutrition facts and similar foods are below.

How much exercise is that?

For a 60 kg adult, 130 kcal is roughly 35 minutes of brisk walking or 18 minutes of jogging. Against a 2,000 kcal day, this serving is about 6% of the total.
