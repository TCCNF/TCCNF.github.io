Irish coffee calories

Irish coffee (1 cup (200ml)) has about 234 kcal. Nutrition facts and similar foods are below.

234kcal per cup (200ml)

2.1 gProtein
10 gFat
0 gFibre
6.5 gSaturated fat
0.1 gTrans fat
3.4 gUnsaturated fat
The split of the 10 g of fat is an estimate: unsaturated fat = total fat minus saturated and trans fat.

How much exercise is that?

For a 60 kg adult, 234 kcal is roughly 64 minutes of brisk walking or 32 minutes of jogging. Against a 2,000 kcal day, this serving is about 12% of the total.
