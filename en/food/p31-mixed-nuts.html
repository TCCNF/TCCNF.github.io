Mixed nuts calories

Mixed nuts (28 g) has about 170 kcal. Nutrition facts and similar foods are below.

Calories from: carbohydrate 13% · protein 11% · fat 75%

How much exercise is that?

For a 60 kg adult, 170 kcal is roughly 46 minutes of brisk walking or 23 minutes of jogging. Against a 2,000 kcal day, this serving is about 8% of the total.
