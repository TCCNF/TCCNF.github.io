Flaxseed calories

Flaxseed (1 tbsp (10g)) has about 55 kcal. Nutrition facts and similar foods are below.

55kcal per tbsp (10g)
3 gCarbohydrate
1.9 gProtein
4.2 gFat
2.8 gFibre
0.4 gSaturated fat
0 gTrans fat
3.8 gUnsaturated fat
The split of the 4.2 g of fat is an estimate: unsaturated fat = total fat minus saturated and trans fat.
Calories from: carbohydrate 21% · protein 13% · fat 66%

How much exercise is that?

For a 60 kg adult, 55 kcal is roughly 15 minutes of brisk walking or 7 minutes of jogging. Against a 2,000 kcal day, this serving is about 3% of the total.

How much protein, carbohydrate and fat is in Flaxseed?
One serving (1 tbsp (10g)) has about 1.9 g protein, 3 g carbohydrate, 4.2 g fat and 2.8 g fibre. Of the fat, about 0.4 g is saturated, 0 g trans and 3.8 g unsaturated.
