One-dish rice takeaway box calories

One-dish rice takeaway box (1 box) has on average about 630 kcal. Nutrition facts and similar foods are below.

This is an average. One-dish rice takeaway box has no fixed contents, so the real figure depends on the dishes, portion and oil, and can differ by a few hundred kcal: steamed, blanched and light-sauce dishes come in much lower, fried, braised and saucy ones much higher.
